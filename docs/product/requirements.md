# Product requirements

NSoft Mail Companion is an independent Apache-2.0 management application for hosting custom-domain business mail alongside Coolify or standalone Docker. M Suthakaran is the legal copyright owner, trading as NSoft Academy. Upstream components retain their licenses.

Platform administrators manage tenants and domain administrators; domain administrators manage only their tenant's domains and mailboxes. Mailbox users use standard email clients and per-domain Roundcube webmail. Support explicit sending enablement, strong authentication, persistent messages/queues, DNS assistance, delivery diagnostics and recovery tooling. No billing or artificial paid limits.

Success means tested tenant isolation, no unauthenticated relay, authenticated sender ownership, usable domain/webmail onboarding, reliable local SMTP/IMAP delivery, safe configuration retries and preserved data across deployments. Production acceptance additionally requires actual provider connectivity, DNS/TLS, external receiver authentication, capacity testing and off-server restoration. Inbox folder placement is outside the software's control.
